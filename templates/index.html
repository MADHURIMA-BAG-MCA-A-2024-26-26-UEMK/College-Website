About Our College

Raja Narendra Lal Khan Women's College is committed to providing quality education, fostering intellectual growth and empowering young women to build a brighter future. The college offers a supportive environment for academic excellence, skill development and holistic growth.

Established in 1957 on the historic grounds of the Gope Palace, our institution stands as an emblem of academic distinction, women's liberation, and cultural heritage in West Bengal.
