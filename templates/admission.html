Four-Step Admission Process

Transparent, merit-based admission procedure adhering to University Grants Commission and West Bengal Higher Education Department guidelines.

1

Online Application

Fill in the centralized online application form, select desired undergraduate/postgraduate programs, and upload scanned documents.

2

Merit List Publication

Comprehensive provisional and final subject-wise merit lists are published based strictly on marks scored in qualifying examinations.

3

Document Verification

Shortlisted candidates undergo in-person verification of original certificates, mark sheets, and caste/EWS credentials at the college desk.

4

Fee Payment & Induction

Secure admission confirmation by paying the semester fees online, followed by the collegiate orientation program.

Eligibility Criteria

Minimum qualifications for admission into major degree programs for session 2026-27:

Course Level Duration Minimum Qualification Required
BCA Undergraduate 3 Years 10+2 Higher Secondary in any stream with Mathematics / Computer Science / Statistics (Min. 50% aggregate).
MCA Postgraduate 2 Years BCA / B.Sc in Comp Sci / IT or Bachelor's with Mathematics at 10+2 or degree level (Min. 50% + valid JECA).
B.Ed Professional (NCTE) 2 Years Bachelor's or Master's degree in Sciences/Humanities with minimum 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/PWD).
B.A. (Honours) Undergraduate (NEP) 4 Years 10+2 passed from recognized Council/Board with minimum 45% aggregate and 50% in chosen major discipline.
B.Sc (Honours) Undergraduate (NEP) 4 Years 10+2 passed in Science stream with Physics, Chemistry, Math/Biology (Min. 50% aggregate & subject cut-off).
Other Diploma/PG PG / Vocational 1 to 2 Years Graduation / 10+2 passed depending on specialized certification course guidelines.

Required Documents Checklist

  • 10th Standard Admit Card / Birth Certificate (Age Proof)
  • 10+2 Marksheet & Passing Certificate
  • Graduation Marksheet (For MCA, B.Ed, M.A./M.Sc applicants)
  • Caste / EWS / PWD Certificate (if applicable)
  • Recent passport size photographs (scanned copy & 4 prints)
  • Aadhaar Card photocopy
  • School/College Leaving & Migration Certificate
