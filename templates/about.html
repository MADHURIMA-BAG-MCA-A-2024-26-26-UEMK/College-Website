Heritage of Excellence

Empowering Generations of Women Since 1957

Nestled within the regal environs of the historic Gope Palace in Medinipur, Raja Narendra Lal Khan Women's College was consecrated in 1957 by eminent freedom fighters, visionaries, and social reformers dedicated to lighting the torch of women's education in the historic district of Midnapore.

Conferred with prestigious Autonomous Status by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and re-accredited with 'A' Grade (Cycle-3) by NAAC, our institution blends historic architecture with state-of-the-art computational laboratories, research facilities, and forward-looking pedagogical frameworks under the National Education Policy.

Guiding Principles

Vision & Mission

Our core philosophy is distilled in our timeless Sanskrit motto: "Sa Vidya Ya Vimuktaye" — True education is that which liberates the mind and spirit.

Our Vision

To be an internationally recognized center of academic eminence that elevates women into independent thinkers, ethical innovators, and transformative leaders who steer social justice, scientific discovery, and societal well-being.

Our Mission

To impart affordable, quality higher education with modern technological competencies, cultivate critical inquiry, instill ethical civic values, and empower young women from all socio-economic strata through inclusive mentorship.

Campus Amenities

World-Class Infrastructure

A 45-acre sprawling campus equipped with cutting-edge academic, research, and recreational facilities.

Central Digital Library

Over 65,000 volumes, automated barcode checkout, INFLIBNET N-LIST e-journal subscriptions, and quiet reading zones.

High-Tech Computing Labs

Over 200 high-speed computers, dedicated gigabit optical fiber connection, AI/ML clusters, and full-stack development suites.

DST-FIST Science Laboratories

Advanced instruments for spectroscopic analysis, molecular biology tissue culture, spectrophotometers, and botanical greenhouse.

Sports Complex & Gym

Standard 400m athletic track, multi-gym for women scholars, badminton courts, table tennis arena, and basketball courts.

Secure On-Campus Hostels

Four well-appointed hostel blocks accommodating 600+ students with 24/7 CCTV surveillance, nutritious dining, and solar water heaters.

Eco-Friendly Green Campus

Solar roof installations, extensive medicinal plant garden, rainwater harvesting reservoirs, and zero-plastic policies.
